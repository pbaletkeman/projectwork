# Phase 2 - Vertical Slice

## Objective

Transform the prototype into a small but complete gameplay experience.

The goal is to prove:

"I can build one complete level."

---

## New Features

Player gains:

- Health UI
- Secondary ability
- Checkpoint system
- Pause menu

World gains:

- One complete biome
- Environmental hazards
- Locked door
- Unlockable ability

Enemies gain:

- Melee enemy
- Ranged enemy

---

## New Ability

Light Shield

Use:

- Blocks damage
- Required to cross hazard areas

This introduces Metroidvania-style progression.

---

## Content

Build:

- 1 biome
- 3 enemy types
- 1 miniboss
- 1 boss

Expected playtime:

20-30 minutes

---

## Technical Goals

Learn:

- State Machines
- Scene Inheritance
- Resource Files
- Save/Load Basics

---

## Time Budget

Maximum:

100 hours

---

## Definition Of Done

A stranger can:

- Start game
- Complete biome
- Defeat boss
- Unlock ability
- Reach ending

Without assistance.
